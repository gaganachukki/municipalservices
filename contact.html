Frequently Asked Questions

How can I pay my property tax online? +

You can pay your property tax by navigating to Services -> Property Tax. Enter your assessment number, verify the details, and proceed to payment using Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking, or UPI.

How long does it take to get a Birth Certificate? +

Once the hospital initiates the record and you submit the required documents online, the digital certificate is usually generated within 3-5 working days. You will receive an SMS alert once ready for download.

How do I track my complaint status? +

Login to your citizen dashboard and go to "My Complaints". You can view real-time updates and remarks from the assigned municipal officer.
