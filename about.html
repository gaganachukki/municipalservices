About Municipality

Dedicated to transparent and effective urban governance.

Our History & Evolution

Established in 1950, our municipality has grown from a small administrative body to a large-scale governance structure managing the needs of over 1 million residents. Our journey has been marked by continuous adaptation to modern technologies and expanding urban limits.

Core Values & Principles

Transparency

Open data policies and transparent decision-making processes accessible to every citizen.

Innovation

Embracing smart city technologies to solve urban challenges efficiently.

Sustainability

Committed to green initiatives, renewable energy, and eco-friendly urban planning.

Inclusivity

Ensuring equal opportunities and accessible services for citizens from all walks of life.

Vision

To be the most advanced, clean, and citizen-friendly municipality by integrating technology, sustainable practices, and inclusive development to ensure a high standard of living for all citizens.

Mission

Delivering timely and transparent public services, maintaining robust urban infrastructure, protecting the environment, and actively engaging citizens in the city's governance and decision-making processes.

Future Vision 2030

Our roadmap for the next decade focuses on hyper-connectivity, zero-emission transit, and fully automated civic services.

  • 100% EV Public Transit: Complete transition of the city bus fleet to electric vehicles by 2028.
  • AI Traffic Management: Neural-network-based traffic lights to reduce commute times by 30%.
  • Smart Water Grid: IoT sensors on all municipal pipelines to eliminate water leakage and track purity.

Community Engagement

A smart city is built by its citizens. We provide multiple avenues for active participation.

Digital Townhalls

Monthly virtual meetings with the Mayor and council members to discuss neighborhood issues directly.

Youth Civic Council

A dedicated platform for students and young professionals to propose innovative urban solutions.

Volunteer Programs

City-wide initiatives for tree planting, lake restoration, and community welfare programs.

Administration Hierarchy

City Council

The legislative body comprising elected representatives from various wards, responsible for policy making.

Executive Wing

Headed by the Municipal Commissioner, responsible for implementing policies and day-to-day administration.

Zonal Offices

Decentralized offices across the city to ensure rapid service delivery and Grievance.
