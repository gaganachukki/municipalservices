Citizen Services

Apply, track, and manage all your municipal requirements online.

Birth Certificate

Register new births and apply for digital birth certificates. Also supports name inclusion and corrections for existing records.

Required Documents:
  • Hospital Discharge Summary
  • Parents ID Proof (Aadhaar/Voter ID)
  • Marriage Certificate (Optional)

Death Certificate

Register deaths within municipal limits and obtain digital death certificates for legal and financial purposes.

Required Documents:
  • Doctor's Certificate / Hospital Intimation
  • Deceased's ID Proof
  • Applicant's ID Proof

Property Tax

Calculate, view, and pay your half-yearly or annual property taxes. Download payment receipts instantly.

Requirements:
  • Property Assessment Number
  • Old Tax Receipt

Trade License

Apply for a new trade license or renew existing ones. Compulsory for all commercial establishments operating in city limits.

Required Documents:
  • Rental Agreement / Property Tax Receipt
  • Owner's ID Proof
  • NOC from Fire Department (if applicable)

Building Approval

Submit building plans for automated scrutiny and approval. Track application status and pay scrutiny fees online.

Required Documents:
  • Architectural Drawings (CAD)
  • Property Title Deed
  • Structural Stability Certificate

📢 Complaint Registration

Report issues related to street lights, garbage collection, water supply, or damaged roads. Real-time status tracking available.

Requirements:
  • Ward / Street details
  • Photographic evidence (Optional)
  • Contact number for updates

How It Works

3. Receive Output
