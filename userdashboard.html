Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my property tax payment?

You can track all successful and pending payments in the 'My Payments' tab. A PDF receipt is generated instantly upon successful transaction.

What documents are required for a Trade License?

You will need ID proof, address proof of the business premises, property tax receipt, and a NOC from the building owner.

How long does it take to process a Birth Certificate?

Standard processing time is 7-10 working days after the hospital submits the required documents to the municipal portal.
